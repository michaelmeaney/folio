# Mosaic Presentation Design System

Version: 0.1.0

## Lineage

Mosaic is an original Folio system informed by modular productivity interfaces and Microsoft Fluent's published principles around hierarchy, spacing, neutral surfaces, semantic colour and adaptable grids. It is not a Microsoft template and uses no Microsoft marks or proprietary assets.

## Principles

- Build hierarchy through regions, spacing and proximity.
- Make complex systems approachable without making them childish.
- Use neutral surfaces as the foundation; colour has semantic purpose.
- Components may repeat, but composition must still have a dominant idea.
- Favour familiar patterns and predictable alignment.
- Accessibility and legibility outrank decoration.

## Composition

Use a 12-column grid and a consistent spacing ramp. Regions may combine columns and rows into modules. Important information receives more area, not merely stronger colour. Use whitespace to separate conceptual groups before adding lines or containers.

## Typography

Use Aptos for portability. Sentence case by default. Semibold creates hierarchy. Left-align analytical content. Centre only short hero statements or a single metric.

## Colour

Neutral surfaces dominate. Brand indigo identifies the primary narrative thread. Supporting cyan, teal and purple are available for genuinely distinct categories. Semantic colours communicate status and should not be decorative.

## Components

Mosaic permits more componentisation than Lumen or Meridian: metric tiles, compact comparison regions, status strips and modular diagrams are valid. Keep surfaces flat, radii restrained and borders subtle. Avoid turning every concept into a card.

## Charts and diagrams

Use clear legends or direct labels, consistent category colours and visible units. Architecture diagrams may use modular regions to express platform boundaries. Keep connectors orthogonal where this improves scanability.

## Depth

Use depth only to establish layering or focus. Avoid ornamental shadows. A raised surface must have a semantic reason to sit above another.

## Prohibited patterns

No copied Microsoft logos, Fluent illustrations or proprietary templates; no gratuitous four-colour Windows motifs; no excessive cards, deep shadows, rainbow status coding, tiny UI screenshots or decorative gradients.
