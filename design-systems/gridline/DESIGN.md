# Gridline Presentation Design System

Version: 0.1.0

## Lineage

Gridline is an original Folio technical system informed by IBM's public Carbon design-system emphasis on grids, neutral typography, functional colour and systematic information design. It does not reproduce IBM marks, proprietary templates or Carbon components.

## Principles

- Make the underlying system visible.
- Structure is a design element: grid, baseline, alignment and hierarchy should be obvious.
- Prefer exact diagrams and data over atmospheric illustration.
- Colour communicates role, state or focus; it is not decoration.
- Technical density is acceptable when relationships remain inspectable.
- Labels, identifiers and units matter.

## Grid

Use a 16-column grid with narrow gutters. Major regions align rigorously. Fine rules may remain visible when they clarify structure. Large headings may align to the same column system rather than floating independently.

## Typography

Use neutral sans typography and a monospaced face for code, identifiers, paths, protocols and machine-readable values. Large headings may use regular weight; scale and placement create hierarchy. Avoid overusing bold.

## Colour

Near-black and cool greys dominate. Blue is the primary focus colour. Additional colours are semantic or categorical and must remain consistent across a deck. Running text stays neutral.

## Technical diagrams

Prefer explicit trust boundaries, interfaces, protocols, identifiers and directional connectors. Orthogonal routing is preferred for architecture. Use line weight and fill to distinguish boundary, flow and emphasis. Do not hide complexity behind decorative illustrations.

## Data visualisation

Use shared axes and small multiples where comparison matters. Show units and baselines. Directly label important series. Gridlines are permitted when they improve quantitative reading.

## Components

Use rectangular regions, tabs, labels, code strips and technical callouts. Corner radii should be minimal or absent. Do not imitate application UI unless the UI itself is the subject.

## Prohibited patterns

No IBM logos, proprietary IBM templates, copied Carbon components, soft atmospheric blobs, gratuitous 3D objects, oversized radii, glass effects, ambiguous arrows or unlabeled architecture nodes.
