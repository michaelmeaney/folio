# Werk Presentation Design System

Version: 0.1.0

## Lineage

Werk is an original Folio geometric-modernist system inspired by Bauhaus pedagogy, functional typography and later International Style composition. Historical references inform the grammar; no institutional marks or reproduced artworks are included.

## Principles

- Form follows communicative function.
- Reduce compositions to type, line, plane and basic geometry.
- Use asymmetry intentionally; balance visual mass rather than centring everything.
- Primary colours are scarce structural accents, not a requirement to use all three.
- Typography participates in the composition.
- Decoration without informational or compositional purpose is removed.

## Composition

Use a strict grid, but permit deliberate tension through asymmetric placement, cropped geometry and strong directional axes. Circles, squares, rectangles and triangles may establish hierarchy, sequence or grouping. They must carry compositional meaning.

## Typography

Use bold grotesk-style sans typography. Headlines can become graphic elements through scale, rotation or cropping, but body text remains conventionally readable. All-caps is allowed for short labels and exhibit identifiers, not paragraphs.

## Colour

Default to warm paper and black. Red, blue and yellow are the core chromatic accents. A normal slide should use one primary accent; two are acceptable when encoding contrast. Three-colour compositions are reserved for deliberate hero or section slides.

## Geometry

Geometry can replace containers: a circle may mark a focal number; a bar can establish sequence; a coloured plane can divide narrative regions. Prefer hard edges and flat fills. No shadows, glass, blur or soft gradients.

## Charts and diagrams

Use strong axes, direct labels and geometric marks. Simplify charts aggressively. Diagrams should feel constructed rather than illustrated. Connectors are straight or orthogonal; arrowheads are simple.

## Imagery

Photography may be monochrome, duotone-like or strongly cropped when legally available. Never apply historical Bauhaus artworks as decorative backgrounds merely to signal the style.

## Prohibited patterns

No Bauhaus logos or reproduced artworks, no pseudo-vintage distress, no decorative primary-colour confetti, no gradients, no shadows, no soft 3D forms, no pill-shaped UI, and no arbitrary geometric clutter.
