# Aperture Presentation Design System

Version: 0.1.0

## Lineage

Aperture is an original Folio system inspired by premium product communication and Apple's content-first restraint. It does not reproduce Apple brand assets, proprietary fonts, templates or trade dress.

## Principles

- Content is the hero; branding and decoration recede.
- Use large scale, exceptional whitespace and a small number of precisely aligned objects.
- Prefer one dominant visual or statement over collections of equal-weight elements.
- Colour is functional and scarce. Neutral surfaces carry most slides.
- Product imagery may be cinematic; diagrams remain simple and exact.
- Typography should feel calm rather than editorially aggressive.

## Composition

Use a 12-column grid but allow hero content to break it deliberately. Most slides should contain one dominant object occupying 45-70% of the visual field. Titles are short. Supporting copy should rarely exceed 45 words.

Centred composition is allowed for title, hero and reveal slides. Analytical slides revert to left alignment. Preserve broad empty margins and avoid edge-to-edge information density.

## Typography

Use the token ramp. Headlines may be large and compact. Body copy remains regular weight. Use semibold to create hierarchy rather than colour. Avoid all-caps, condensed text, decorative serif faces and more than three type sizes on a normal slide.

## Colour and surfaces

Default to warm light grey or white. Use the blue accent for a single focal action, datum or annotation. Do not create multicolour category systems unless the content requires them. Never use gradients merely to make a slide feel premium.

## Imagery

Prefer isolated objects, high-quality product/detail photography, subtle material studies and simple spatial renders. Allow generous negative space. Never place stock-office photography behind text.

## Charts and diagrams

Reduce charts to the marks necessary to communicate the point. Highlight one series; mute context. Remove redundant legends, borders and gridlines. Diagrams should use thin neutral connectors and a single accent for focus.

## Containers

Avoid card grids. When a surface is necessary, use a white field with generous internal spacing, subtle radius and either no border or a hairline border. Shadows are exceptional.

## Prohibited patterns

No glassmorphism imitation, rainbow gradients, excessive rounded rectangles, decorative device chrome, fake Apple logos, proprietary Apple typography, dense dashboards, tiny footnotes or ornamental icon rows.
