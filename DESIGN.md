# Folio design guidance index

Folio has no global presentation style. Select the execution mode and transformation intent using [the canonical Skill](skills/folio/SKILL.md), then follow its [composition contract](skills/folio/references/composition-contract.md).

Quick preserves source styling. Guided applies local preferences. Governed resolves a named or supplied system through [design-system resolution](skills/folio/references/design-system-resolution.md) and its manifest. Naming a system does not authorise changing the reference composition.

For explicitly selected Lumen, begin with [its manifest](design-systems/lumen/system.json), which declares [Lumen guidance](design-systems/lumen/DESIGN.md) and canonical resources. Legacy files under `design/` are compatibility material and do not confer authority on Quick, Guided or another system.

Apply the all-mode accessibility checks in [acceptance](skills/folio/references/acceptance.md). Lumen's [functional contrast decision](docs/design/design-001-lumen-functional-contrast.md) documents its corresponding tokens and treatment rules.
